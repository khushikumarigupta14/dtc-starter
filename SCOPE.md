# Scope

Colourpalet sells original drawings/paintings, accepts custom artwork enquiries, and lets authenticated vendors submit artwork for operator approval. MVP covers home, gallery, artwork detail, cart, checkout, confirmation, artist/about, custom request, contact, customer account, customer orders, and a vendor submission dashboard, delivered through the phases in `ROADMAP.md`.

Commerce includes catalog, images, collections/categories, INR pricing, inventory, shipping, COD, order management, customers, and sold state. Custom artwork requests are enquiries, not cart items or automatically created orders.

The vendor MVP covers artwork submission, ownership, review status, rejection notes, and admin-approved publication. Vendor payouts, commissions, vendor order fulfilment, vendor analytics, vendor storefront pages, and self-service publication remain out of scope.

Online payments, alternative databases/platforms, auctions, subscriptions, loyalty, wallets, gift cards, live chat, advanced CRM, AI art generation, native apps, multi-language/currency, and production cloud storage are out of MVP scope unless explicitly requested.
