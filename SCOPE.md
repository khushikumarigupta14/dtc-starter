# Scope

Colourpalet sells original drawings/paintings and accepts custom artwork enquiries. MVP covers home, gallery, artwork detail, cart, checkout, confirmation, artist/about, custom request, contact, customer account, and customer orders, delivered through the phases in `ROADMAP.md`.

Commerce includes catalog, images, collections/categories, INR pricing, inventory, shipping, COD, order management, customers, and sold state. Custom artwork requests are enquiries, not cart items or automatically created orders.

Online payments, alternative databases/platforms, marketplaces, auctions, subscriptions, loyalty, wallets, gift cards, live chat, advanced CRM, AI art generation, native apps, multi-language/currency, and production cloud storage are out of MVP scope unless explicitly requested.
